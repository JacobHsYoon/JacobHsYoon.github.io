About

Hi! I'm Jacob, an Electrical Engineering student at Northeastern University with a minor in Robotics. My interests are in embedded systems, robotics hardware, and PCB design, especially projects that connect control logic with real physical behavior.

My recent work includes STM32-based actuator control, motor driver PCB design, and sensor integration for robotics systems. I enjoy building hardware that can be tested, measured, and improved through careful debugging and validation. I also bring hands-on troubleshooting experience from network systems maintenance and field engineering work in the Republic of Korea Air Force.
